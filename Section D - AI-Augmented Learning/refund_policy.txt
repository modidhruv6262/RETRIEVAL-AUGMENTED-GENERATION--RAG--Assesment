FOOD DELIVERY REFUND AND CANCELLATION POLICY

1. Cancellation Rules: Customers can cancel an order within 60 seconds of placing it without any penalty. After 60 seconds, if the restaurant has accepted the order and started preparing the food, a 100% cancellation fee applies.

2. Late Deliveries: Orders delayed by more than 45 minutes past the estimated delivery window qualify for a full delivery fee refund and a 50 rupee discount coupon for the next order.

3. Missing Items: If any ordered item is missing from a sealed delivery package, report it within 2 hours of delivery with an itemized photo of the received items. A full refund for the missing item will be processed within 3 to 5 business days.

4. Damaged or Spoiled Food: In cases of severe spillage, contamination, or unhygienic food condition, submit clear photos to customer support within 2 hours. A 100% refund of the total order value including taxes will be issued.

5. Payment Methods and Refund Timeline: Refunds are issued to the original payment source. UPI and wallet refunds reflect within 24 hours. Credit and debit card refunds require 5 to 7 business banking days depending on the issuing bank.
