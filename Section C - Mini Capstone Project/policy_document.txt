QUICKBITE FOOD DELIVERY PLATFORM COMPREHENSIVE POLICY AND SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT

SECTION 1: ORDER PLACEMENT AND DISPATCH SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENTS
QuickBite is dedicated to delivering fresh, high-quality meals with prompt operational precision. Upon order submission through the consumer mobile application or web portal, our automated kitchen routing system instantly transfers the ticket to the selected restaurant partner. Standard delivery timeframes range between thirty and forty-five minutes from the moment of merchant confirmation. During peak operational periods such as weekend evenings, major festive events, or hazardous inclement weather (including heavy monsoon downpours or severe heatwaves), the standard delivery timeframe may be reasonably extended to sixty minutes to ensure the safety of our delivery fleet. If an order exceeds sixty minutes without prior automated communication or dispatch notification, the customer becomes eligible for a priority compensation voucher equivalent to the delivery fee. Delivery riders are strictly mandated to wait at the customer's designated drop-off address for up to ten minutes while attempting phone contact. If the customer fails to answer or receive the parcel within ten minutes, the driver is instructed to mark the order as undelivered due to customer unavailability, and no complimentary re-delivery will be provided.

SECTION 2: ORDER CANCELLATION RULES AND MERCHANT RESTITUTION
We understand that sudden schedule shifts occur. Customers maintain the right to cancel an order completely free of charge within sixty seconds of initial submission. Once sixty seconds have elapsed and the merchant partner accepts the order and initiates ingredient preparation in the kitchen, order cancellation through the application interface is restricted. If a customer demands cancellation after kitchen food preparation has commenced, a mandatory cancellation fee equal to one hundred percent of the total food subtotal is charged. This fee is disbursed directly to our merchant partner to cover non-recoverable food ingredients, kitchen labor, and packaging overhead. If an order is canceled unilaterally by the restaurant due to raw ingredient stockouts or kitchen hardware failures, the customer is granted an immediate full refund plus a loyalty discount voucher for their subsequent order.

SECTION 3: COMPREHENSIVE REFUND ELIGIBILITY AND COMPENSATION SCHEDULE
Refund requests are rigorously assessed against verifiable proof and internal telemetry. To maintain eligibility, customers must report all grievances through the in-app support center within one hundred and twenty minutes of the recorded delivery timestamp. In scenarios where one or more dishes are missing from a tamper-evident sealed parcel, the customer must submit a digital ticket specifying the omitted dishes. Following dispatch verification, a full refund representing the exact price of the missing items is credited back to the customer's primary payment method within three to five business banking days. In cases of incorrect deliveries where a customer receives items different from their confirmed order (such as non-vegetarian dishes delivered instead of vegetarian selections), QuickBite provides either an immediate complimentary re-delivery or a one hundred percent refund covering all food costs, taxes, and delivery fees. If food items arrive spoiled, unhygienic, or severely damaged due to transit mishandling, photographic proof must be uploaded. Upon confirmation by our automated compliance team, a full refund is executed.

SECTION 4: PRICING, SURCHARGES, AND MODIFICATIONS
All menu item prices displayed on QuickBite are determined by respective merchant partners. A nominal platform service fee of five to ten rupees is applied per transaction to fund round-the-clock customer support infrastructure. In situations where an ordered beverage or side dish is temporarily unavailable, merchant staff will contact the customer to confirm an equivalent substitution prior to kitchen dispatch.
